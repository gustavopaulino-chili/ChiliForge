LANDING PAGE FINAL QUALITY CHECKLIST
====================================

KEYWORDS:
quality-check, final-check, mobile, conversion, CTA, accessibility, polish,
landing-page-validation, brandPersonality, toneOfVoice, visual-direction,
modern-design, brand-alignment, layout-variety, contrast


CONVERSION CHECK
----------------

- The visitor understands the offer within 5 seconds.
- The primary CTA is visible above the fold.
- CTA text matches the conversion goal.
- Each section has a clear purpose.
- Objections are handled before the final CTA.
- No unsupported testimonials, metrics, or guarantees were invented.


MODERN DESIGN CHECK
-------------------

- The page looks like a current (2024-2025) brand site, not a 2015 template.
- A clear type hierarchy with a confident, large hero headline.
- Generous whitespace and consistent spacing rhythm (no cramped sections).
- A single coherent depth language (shadows/radius/borders) reused throughout —
  not heavy gradient + glass + shadow stacked on everything.
- One accent color used purposefully for CTAs/highlights; neutral surfaces elsewhere.
- At least one contrast moment (a dark or brand-colored band).
- Tasteful micro-interactions (hover lift, scroll reveal, focus rings) — not
  autoplay carousels or constant animation.


BRAND ALIGNMENT CHECK
---------------------

- The company's brand colors are actually applied (hero, CTAs, accents) — visible,
  not just mentioned in copy.
- The brand's heading/body fonts are used.
- Tone of voice and brand personality visibly shaped layout, not only words.
- The logo appears in nav/footer (and is never used as a hero/section image).
- One dominant visual direction — not an averaged generic SaaS look.


VISUAL CHECK
------------

- Page feels brand-specific, not generic.
- Sections do not all look identical; at least 3 distinct layout archetypes used.
- Hero, proof, benefits, features, and final CTA do NOT all use the same card grid.
- Images are treated intentionally (cover/contain, overlays where needed).
- CTA styling is consistent across the page.
- The first viewport clearly signals the product/service/place/brand.


MOBILE CHECK
------------

- Hero is readable on mobile; headline scales down, doesn't shrink the layout.
- Multi-column grids collapse to 1 column.
- CTA is easy to tap (>= 44px); sticky nav CTA reachable.
- Text does not overflow; nothing is cut off at ~360px width.
- Images do not crush content; section spacing is balanced.
- Forms are usable on small screens.


ACCESSIBILITY CHECK
-------------------

- Text contrast meets WCAG AA on its actual background.
- Buttons/links are identifiable and have visible focus states.
- Form fields have labels; icon-only buttons have aria-labels.
- One logical heading order; single <h1>.
- Page works without relying only on hover; motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.


COPY CHECK
----------

- Headline is specific and outcome-first.
- Subheadline supports the headline.
- Benefits are outcome-focused; features are tied to value.
- CTA is short and action-oriented.
- No filler sections.


FAIL AND REVISE CONDITIONS
--------------------------

Revise before final output when:
- the page could fit any generic company after changing the logo
- tone/personality/preferredStyle do not visibly affect the layout
- every section is a card grid / the page has only one layout pattern
- the hero is vague or does not show the actual offer/product/service
- brand colors/fonts were provided but are not visibly applied
- CTAs are visually weak or inconsistent
- visual assets are provided but not used meaningfully
- the design relies on stacked heavy effects (gradient + glass + big shadows) that
  look busy instead of polished
